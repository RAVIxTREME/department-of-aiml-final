About the Department

A centre of excellence in emerging technology education

Department of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning is established in 2023 with an intake of 60 students. The aim of the department is to establish a state of the art knowledge center in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Research which produces engineers imbibed with the spirit of professionalism & responsible citizenship.

The department has well-equipped laboratories, highly qualified & experienced & devoted team of teaching faculty members. Conducive learning environment exists in the department for both students and staff. High-quality technical education keeping in mind the industrial requirement is provided to the students in the areas of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in order to meet the challenges of the industries.

The department has a Students mentoring Scheme to assist the students in their learning difficulties and to monitor their academic performance regularly. Mentors interact with students and offer them emotional as well as academic support along with motivation.

The department caters to the needs of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning sector by imparting a requisite knowledge to the students, in the field of software development, Java Programming, Software Engineering, Data Communication and Computer Networks, Microprocessors and Data Structure using Python and many advanced topics.

Vision & Mission

Guiding principles of the department

Vision

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AN) Department strives to educate students to become Industry ready Engineers having Professional attitude and groomed personality.

Mission

  • M1 : To provide well defined system to ensure quality education by strengthening teaching learning processes through innovative practices.
  • M2 : To provide a platform where students are exposed to the Industry, up bridged with the industry standards and requirements.
  • M3 : To train students by teaching them leadership and teamwork skills.
  • M4 : To groom students enriching their personality and social values.

Laboratories & Facilities

Infrastructure that supports hands-on learning

Programming Laboratory
